What's Actually In Your Oil Change

European engines are spec-sensitive. Every RoverRam service uses the exact oil approval and OEM filter your car calls for — confirmed by VIN before I arrive.

🛻 Land Rover

  • Oil spec: 0W-20 / 5W-30 (STJLR.03.5003 / 03.5005)
  • Filter: Genuine / OEM (Mann, Mahle)
  • Capacity: ~7–8 quarts
  • Plug: New crush washer, torqued to spec

🚗 Mini Cooper

  • Oil spec: BMW Longlife-01 / LL-04 (0W-30 / 5W-30)
  • Filter: Genuine / OEM (Mann, Mahle)
  • Capacity: ~4.2–4.8 quarts
  • Plug: New crush washer, torqued to spec

🏎️ Audi

  • Oil spec: VW 502.00 / 504.00 / 507.00
  • Filter: Genuine / OEM (Mann, Mahle, Bosch)
  • Capacity: ~5–6.5 quarts
  • Plug: New crush washer, torqued to spec

🏁 Porsche

  • Oil spec: Porsche A40 (C30/C20 on newer DFI)
  • Filter: Genuine / OEM (Mahle, Mann)
  • Capacity: ~8–10 quarts (dry-sump)
  • Plug: New crush washer, torqued to spec

Every visit also includes a quick visual health check (fluids, belts, leaks) and responsible recycling of your old oil.
